1. Executive Summary
The existing site presents RedKross Psychology as the psychology and mental-wellbeing wing of RedKross Research Foundation. It
describes counselling, student and career counselling, support groups, community outreach, Psychological First Aid training,
psychology research, internships, workshops, community activities, Creative Corner, educational resources, a blog, FAQs and contact
options.
The redesign should be warm, accessible, culturally sensitive and clinically responsible. Visitors must understand what support is
offered, who provides it, how to enquire, what it costs or how sliding-scale options work, and what to do in an emergency.
Core principle: preserve useful breadth, organise around visitor needs, and label what is available now versus planned.
Primary goals
 Help people find the right support or information without feeling judged.
 
 Explain services, formats, eligibility, costs, availability and limitations accurately.
 
 Enable counselling enquiries without collecting sensitive clinical details in a generic form.
 
 Present internships and training honestly, including supervision, prerequisites, hours and certificate conditions.
 
 Help institutions request workshops and outreach.
 
 Support responsible psychology research and community education.
 
 Protect privacy, accessibility, safeguarding and emergency signposting.

5. Detailed Page Requirements
5.1 About — /about
 Keep the existing story: RedKross began as a technology research organisation with a belief that knowledge should serve people; the
 psychology wing emerged in response to needs for accessible, stigma-free support.
 Explain mission, vision and values: confidentiality within clear boundaries; access and affordability; evidence over fads; local capacity;
 stigma-free care; transparency.
 Publish practitioner credentials and biographies only after verification and consent.

5.2 Services — /services
 Show six programmes with audience, format, what is offered, eligibility, availability and action.
 
 Separate direct support from training, community outreach and research.
 
 Use statuses such as Available, Enquiry Required, Planned or Temporarily Unavailable only when accurate.
 
5.3 Counselling — /counselling
 Explain individual and student/career counselling in plain, non-stigmatising language.
 
 State session length, online/in-person availability, service region, fees/sliding-scale process, provider qualifications and booking steps
 only when verified.
 Explain limits to confidentiality before service begins.
 
 Do not collect detailed symptoms, diagnoses, trauma narratives or medical records through a general contact form.
 
5.4 Support Groups — /support-groups
 Describe actual group themes such as grief, caregiver stress, anxiety management or young-adult wellbeing only if currently
 facilitated.
 State group size, facilitator, schedule, cost, eligibility and intake only when confirmed.
 
 Explain that confidentiality among group members cannot be guaranteed like a professional’s own duty of confidentiality; publish
 group agreements and safety/referral process.
5.5 Workshops — /workshops
 Retain the catalogue: Mental Health Literacy 101; Exam Stress & Study Skills; Psychological First Aid; Digital Wellbeing; Workplace
 Mental Wellbeing; Train-the-Trainer / Peer Support Champions.
 For each: audience, outcomes, duration, format, languages, group size, facilitator requirements, certificate conditions and enquiry
 CTA.
 Use WHO-aligned wording only if curriculum is genuinely mapped and reviewed; do not imply WHO endorsement. Clarify participation
 certificate versus professional qualification.
5.6 Internship — /internship
 Keep Foundation Track (4 weeks, undergraduate), Practice Track (8 weeks, final-year UG/postgraduate) and Research Track (8–12
 weeks, postgraduate/research aspirants) only if delivery capacity is confirmed.
 Explain objectives, tasks, supervision, hours, mode, eligibility, selection, cost/stipend status, attendance and assessment.
 
 Interns must not independently diagnose, counsel or access identifiable client data unless specifically permitted under professional
 supervision and applicable rules.
 Certificates/recommendations depend on clear completion criteria. Co-authorship is never guaranteed; it follows genuine contribution
 and authorship standards.
5.7 Community and Activities — /community, /activities
 Explain how schools, colleges and community groups request programmes.
 
 Show only verified activities and dates: camps, campus drives, caregiver circle, mindful mornings, World Mental Health Day and
 volunteer training.
 Do not show recurring schedules unless a coordinator can maintain them. Obtain consent for photos, especially involving minors or
 vulnerable participants.
5.8 Creative Corner — /creative-corner
 Describe art, writing and storytelling as optional expression and psychoeducation.
 
 Explain submission, anonymity, moderation, publication, withdrawal and copyright permission.
 
 Do not imply creative activities are therapy unless they are actually delivered as a supervised service.
 
5.9 Resources — /resources
 Provide verified helplines, practical guides, service directories and evidence-based self-help resources.
 
 Show source, author/reviewer, date and limitations.
 
 Do not diagnose visitors or present self-assessments as diagnoses. Review helpline numbers and links periodically.
 
5.10 Blog — /blog and /blog/[slug]
 Keep existing topics: exam anxiety; talking to family about mental health; digital wellbeing/doomscrolling.
 
 Every article needs author, reviewer where appropriate, date, update date, references and educational disclaimer.
 
 Avoid miracle-cure claims, fear-mongering and unsupported “proven” language.

6. Critical Safety, Privacy and Clinical Governance
This is mandatory. The site handles potentially sensitive mental-health information and must be designed around safe boundaries, not
only visual presentation.
 Emergency signposting: say clearly that the site is not an emergency/crisis service unless an approved staffed service can support
 that claim.
 Verify helplines before launch. The existing site lists Tele-MANAS 14416 and KIRAN 1800-599-0019 as free, 24×7 options; verify
 current official status and wording.
 Never promise “confidential, always”. Explain actual policy and legal/ethical limits before service begins, including applicable
 safeguarding concerns and imminent risk.
 Verify professional qualifications, registration/licensing where relevant, supervision arrangements and scope of practice.
 
 For minors/adolescents, define guardian consent, assent, confidentiality, safeguarding and parent communication according to
 applicable law and professional guidance.
 Do not collect clinical details through a generic marketing form. Use an appropriate secure intake system if clinical intake is required.
 
 Testimonials must be authentic, consented, privacy-reviewed and approved for exact wording/context. Never invent or simulate client
 testimonials.
 Remove “0+ people reached”, “0+ schools partnered” and “0+ interns trained”; remove “100% confidential”. Publish auditable metrics
 with date/method or no counters.
 Obtain informed consent for photos, especially minors, clients and people at sensitive community events.
 
 Volunteers and interns must not be presented as qualified practitioners or independently provide clinical services.
 
 Human-participant research requires appropriate review, consent, data protection, retention and withdrawal routes.
 
 Only advertise free/sliding-scale care, supervision and specific modalities if the service has current capacity.

Form checklist
 Accessible labels, validation and error messages.
 
 Success screen explains next steps without unachievable response-time promises.
 
 Emails reach a monitored inbox.

 Collect only necessary data and document retention/deletion.
 
 Sensitive counselling details must not go to public analytics or generic marketing tools.

9. SEO, Technical and Content Operations
 Unique title and meta description per page; avoid manipulative targeting of sensitive search intent.
 
 Canonical HTTPS URLs on psychology.redkross.org.in; correct sitemap.xml and robots.txt.
 
 Use structured data only when accurate; no fake reviews, ratings or credentials.
 
 Optimise images/fonts and test mobile performance and core journeys.
 
 Do not send sensitive form contents to analytics or advertising systems.
 
 Assign content owners: clinical reviewer, programme coordinator, web editor and privacy/safeguarding contact.
 
 Review helplines and emergency wording regularly; update services when capacity, fees or eligibility change.
 
 Maintain page inventory with owner, evidence/source, last review and next review date.
 
 Prepare backups, dependency updates, access controls and rollback plan.
